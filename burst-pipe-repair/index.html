Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

When a pipe bursts in Union City or the surrounding area, Union City Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. Technicians on our team assist with finding and closing the water supply, while licensed plumbers we coordinate with handle a proper plumbing repair. Once the water source is stopped, we immediately begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable equipment, removing standing water from all affected areas as quickly as possible.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Burst pipes often damage multiple rooms and multiple levels, because water follows gravity through wall cavities, floor assemblies and ceiling spaces. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. By then, mold may already be established in hidden spaces and structural materials may be significantly weakened. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Consumer tools cannot compare with the scale of professional drying and extraction equipment. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
