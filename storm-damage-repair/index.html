When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Union City

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm

Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Schedule a roof inspection every year so weak spots are repaired before storm season
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
