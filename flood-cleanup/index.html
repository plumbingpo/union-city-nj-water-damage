Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Union City Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Union City to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Daily readings from calibrated moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera tell us when everything is fully dry, which is what keeps mold from growing. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

How to Tell You Need Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level
  • Soft, wet drywall or carpeting that is soaked through

Why Hire a Professional for Flood Cleanup?

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Install backwater valves on sewer lines to stop sewage from backing up into your home
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • Keep gutters and storm drains clear of debris so water can flow properly
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
